## Brand & Style

This design system elevates enterprise cloud procurement and digital transformation consulting into a high-precision, forward-looking digital experience. Designed for enterprise leaders (CPOs, CFOs, CIOs, and VPs of Supply Chain), the aesthetic balances institutional trust with high-velocity cloud engineering.

The style unites **Modern B2B Corporate** with **Refined Technical Glassmorphism**. Dark, deep slate backdrops create an atmosphere of security and operational stability, while electric cyan and vibrant solar amber accents—derived directly from cloud architecture and spend optimization emblems—drive focal emphasis, user engagement, and visual momentum. 

Key attributes:
- **Trustworthy & Architectured:** Structural precision, clean micro-grids, and structured data hierarchy convey deep technical fluency across SAP Ariba, Coupa, and OneStream ecosystems.
- **Luminous & Contemporary:** Ambient inner glow effects, hairline borders, frosted translucent cards, and micro-badges provide an unmistakably modern SaaS polish.
- **Decisive & Metric-Focused:** Clear callout structures, high-contrast KPI counters, and interactive roadmap pipelines (Think → Advise → Build → Run → Optimize).

## Colors

The palette is engineered around dark-mode authority and luminous enterprise accents. 

- **Primary (`#0284C7`) & Tertiary (`#38BDF8`):** Represent digital enterprise cloud architecture, integration pipelines, and primary actionable states. Primary cyan drives standard button fills, primary link states, and interactive focal points, while tertiary electric cyan powers badge halos, focus outlines, and active pipeline indicator rings.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B`):** The radiant solar amber acts as the high-impact conversion accent, reflecting fiscal intelligence, ROI optimization, and executive urgency. Reserved for secondary CTAs, key metric values, audit badges, and status highlights.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` & `#070D1E`):** A deep, cold obsidian navy grounding the layout, reducing eye strain for enterprise operations dashboards and marketing collaterals.
- **Glass & Borders:** `surface-glass` combines with `border-subtle` and `border-glow` to establish a frosted floating UI, replacing heavy drop shadows with luminous optical depth.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies to ensure high legibility, geometric balance, and clean humanist clarity across dense SaaS tables and marketing display modules.

- **Display & Headings:** Set with tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`700` and `800`) to assert consultative confidence and authority.
- **Metric Figures:** The specialized `metric-display` level uses tight line heights and bold weights to emphasize enterprise milestones and quantitative proof points.
- **Body & Captions:** Employs open line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to maintain readability against dark, translucent backdrops.
- **Labels & Micro-Badges:** Feature elevated tracking (`0.05em`) in uppercase or title-case to present tags such as ERP modules and framework phases (`THINK`, `ADVISE`, `BUILD`) with editorial crispness.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a responsive 12-column fluid grid system pinned to a maximum container width of `1280px` (`80rem`).

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Full-width card stacking with ergonomic bottom-sheet patterns for interactive filters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `margin-tablet: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Service matrices adapt into 2x2 modular grids.
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column layout with `margin-desktop: 3rem` and `gutter-desktop: 2rem`. Supports asymmetric layouts, sticky consultative sidebars, and side-by-side metric dashboards.

### Vertical Cadence
- Content sections are spaced using `space-2xl` (mobile) and `space-3xl` (desktop) to ensure breathing room between technical narratives.
- Component internal paddings strictly adhere to `space-sm` for compact tags, `space-md` for inputs/buttons, and `space-lg` to `space-xl` for interactive enterprise feature cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **glassmorphic stratification** and **ambient perimeter glows** rather than heavy, opaque dropshadows.

- **Base Layer (Ground):** Solid canvas in `#070D1E` or `#0F172A`.
- **Card & Container Layer (Mid):** `rgba(19, 31, 55, 0.72)` with a CSS backdrop blur of `12px` to `16px`. Outlined with a continuous 1px hairline border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Hover & Active States (Elevated):** Interactive containers elevate via a subtle top-lit inner glow: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` paired with an outer cyan aura: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(2, 132, 197, 0.25)`.
- **Modal & Floating Overlays:** High-blur glassmorphic background (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`), `rgba(10, 17, 40, 0.88)` fill, framed by a soft gold or cyan hairline border to anchor focus.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape language, balancing soft modern ergonomics with structural corporate enterprise precision.

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Input fields, standard CTA buttons, micro-badges, and inline notification toasts.
- **Large Cards & Modules (1rem / 16px):** Enterprise service cards, metric blocks, customer case-study panels, and lifecycle stage boxes.
- **Feature Modals & Showcase Panels (1.5rem / 24px):** Primary dialogs, hero showcase containers, and interactive solution explorers.
- **Pill Geometry (9999px):** Filter chips, technology category tags (Coupa, SAP Ariba, OneStream), and floating status indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid background using electric cyan (`#0284C7`), text in white (`#F8FAFC`), `border-radius: 0.5rem`, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. On hover: `#0EA5E9` with an outer cyan glow (`0 0 20px rgba(2, 132, 197, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary Action (Consultation/Urgent):** Solid amber background (`#F59E0B`), text in dark navy (`#070D1E`), font weight `600`. On hover: `#D97706` with amber halo reflection.
- **Ghost / Outline:** Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`, text `#F8FAFC`. On hover: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` fill and `border-color: #38BDF8`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Platform Micro-Badges (e.g., Coupa, OneStream, SAP Ariba):** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, background `rgba(2, 132, 197, 0.12)`, text `#38BDF8`, border `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`.
- **Framework Status Chips (Think, Advise, Build):** Subtle neutral fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`), gold accent bullet point (`#F59E0B`), font size `0.75rem`, tracking `0.05em` uppercase.

### Cards & Service Containers
- **Glassmorphic Service Cards:** Translucent slate background (`rgba(19, 31, 55, 0.7)`), backdrop blur `16px`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Internal padding `2rem`.
- **Hover Micro-Interaction:** Subtle upward translation (`-3px`) and border illumination using cyan gradient accent (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.5)`).

### Metrics & Stat Callouts
- Large numeric figure in `metric-display` (`3rem`, weight `800`) colored in radiant solar amber (`#F59E0B`) or gradient from cyan to amber.
- Supporting label set below in `label-md` (`#94A3B8`), accompanied by an upward micro-caret indicator.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height `48px`, background `rgba(7, 13, 30, 0.6)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#64748B`, rounded `0.5rem`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#38BDF8` with a non-blur focus ring `0 0 0 2px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`.
- **Checkboxes & Toggles:** Pill switches with active cyan fill, slider handle in crisp white.

### Process Roadmaps (5-Phase Lifecycle)
- Horizontal step timeline on desktop, collapsing to an accordion list on mobile.
- Connected via a luminous 2px line (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`), with active milestone nodes circled in amber rings.
