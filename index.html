Common Coupa Challenges We Address

A Coupa implementation can be technically complete while business teams still face friction. We bridge the gap between technology go-live and realized operational value.

Our Commitment: We do not deliver generic reports. Every engagement produces a prioritized roadmap, measurable criteria, and sustainable support.
FLAGSHIP POINT SOLUTION 01 document_scanner

Coupa Invoice Soft-Copy Validation & Metadata Quality

When supplier invoice documents (PDFs, scans, attachments) do not match Coupa invoice records, AP teams waste hours chasing discrepancies. Our solution provides a pre-validation quality-control layer that compares document content against approved Coupa metadata, flags mismatches, and classifies exceptions before they disrupt payment cycles.

› Header & Supplier: Legal entity name, remit-to address, tax ID/VAT, and bank alignment
› Commercial & PO: PO number verification, payment terms, currency codes, and delivery references
› Financials & Lines: Subtotal, tax rates, shipping/handling, line-item quantities, and unit price tolerances
Complements AP, tax & Coupa approval controls Request Solution Brief arrow_forward
FLAGSHIP POINT SOLUTION 02 rule

AP Exception Reduction & Touchless Optimization

High exception queues strain AP capacity, delay supplier payments, and risk missing early payment discounts. We perform root-cause analysis across recurring hold codes (price tolerance, quantity mismatch, missing PO, tax variance), tune validation rules, and streamline resolution workflows to dramatically lower manual touches.

› Exception Triage: Root-cause taxonomy of hold codes across top-volume suppliers
› Rule Calibration: Precision tolerance tuning for minor penny/rounding differences
› Continuous Governance: Weekly AP exception dashboards and supplier feedback loops
