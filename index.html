Frequently Asked Questions

How does SkyKloud differ from traditional Big 4 consultancies? expand_more

SkyKloud operates on a boutique pod model. You work directly with principal consultants and certified system architects who actively configure and write integrations—not an army of junior billable analysts. This reduces engagement costs by 40-60% while accelerating delivery velocity.

What is the standard timeline for a Coupa or HubSpot CRM implementation? expand_more

Depending on scope, a standard Coupa core rollout spans 8 to 14 weeks utilizing our pre-built ERP connectors and master data taxonomies. HubSpot and Microsoft Dynamics CRM setups typically deploy in 4 to 8 weeks with phased persona rollouts.

How does the US Growth Partner Network structure engagements? expand_more

We offer 5 tiered models (Entry, Virtual, Growth, Strategic, and Anchor Partner). Engagements combine a low fractional monthly advisory retainer with an agreed success-based revenue share on closed US client business, aligning our economic interests with your growth.

What security and governance standards do you maintain? expand_more

Our technical architectures align with ISO/IEC 27001 : 2022 and SOC 2 Type II controls. All client work is conducted under strict bilateral NDAs with isolated tenant sandboxes and zero vendor lock-in.
